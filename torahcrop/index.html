What it does

One program for Torah, mezuzah and tefillin

The type is detected automatically; every result can be corrected by hand.

Torah scrolls

Every complete column in the photo is straightened and cropped close to the letters — without cutting line starts or ends. The line count is checked (42).

Mezuzot

Straightens even photos taken sideways or upside down, removes the table around the parchment and checks for 22 lines.

Tefillin

Recognises each parsha and puts them in order: קדש → והיה כי יביאך → שמע → והיה אם שמוע. Several pairs go into separate subfolders.

Straight into DUK

Saves into the DUK folders (TorahP, Mezuzoth, Tefillin) numbered 001, 002… — ready for checking.

Scanner mode

With an IRIScan Express sheet-fed scanner, every new mezuzah scan is straightened, cropped and saved automatically.

Warnings

Flags anything suspicious: text touching the photo edge, a wrong number of lines, a parsha identified with low confidence.
