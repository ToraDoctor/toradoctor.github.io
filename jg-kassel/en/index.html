FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I attend a service as a guest?
Yes, guests are very welcome. For security reasons, please register in advance via the office or the registration form and bring photo ID.
When are services held?
Friday evening at 18:00 in spring and summer, 17:00 in autumn and winter; Shabbat morning at 9:30. See Activities for the current schedule.
How can I become a member?
Please contact the community office. We will advise you on requirements and documents.
Do you offer synagogue tours?
Yes, for groups, school classes and individuals by arrangement. Use the form on the Synagogue Tours page.
Can I get a donation receipt?
Yes, we issue a Spendenbescheinigung on request. Please include your address with the transfer. to verify
