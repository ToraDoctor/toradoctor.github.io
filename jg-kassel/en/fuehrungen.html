Synagogue Tours

For groups, school classes and everyone interested.

Get to know the synagogue

A tour of the synagogue on Bremer Straße introduces Jewish religion and traditions, the service and the history of the Jewish Community of Kassel. Questions are very welcome.

Tours are offered by arrangement – for school classes, associations, groups and individuals. They last about 60–90 minutes.

Please bring photo ID. Men are asked to cover their heads in the synagogue; kippot are provided.
